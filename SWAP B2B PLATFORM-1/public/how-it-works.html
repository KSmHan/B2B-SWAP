THE PROCESS

From dead stock to a matched trade

Each step below is what actually happens on the platform — not a simplified summary.

01

Create an account and verify your phone

Sign up with your business phone number. We text a one-time code to confirm it's really you — this is also the number B2B SWAP will text when a trade is confirmed, so it's worth using a number your team actually checks.

02

List your surplus

Add photos, a description, quantity, and condition. If you're open to it, set a cash range you'd accept to balance an uneven trade — this is always optional and shown right on the listing.

  • Publishing is free, with no limit on how many items you list.
  • Your listing is added to the matching network immediately.
  • You can mark it live, in a chain, or completed at any time.
03

Ask the AI agent what you need

Fill in the two pinned fields on the home page — "I have" and "I need" — in plain language. No categories to browse and no forms. The agent reads your request, finds listings that match what you have, and works out what a fair trade path looks like.

04

The agent builds the trade chain

Every listing on B2B SWAP is a node in a network: what a company has, and what it wants back. The agent searches that network for a path from your surplus to what you need.

  • If one company already wants exactly what you have, it's a direct, one-step trade.
  • If not, the agent links listings together — up to 10 companies deep — until the chain closes and you receive what you asked for.
  • Every step shows technical specs, quantity, location, and where the goods can be picked up, so the whole chain is transparent before anyone commits.
05

Confirm interest — and get notified

When you're ready, confirm interest in the chain with one click. B2B SWAP sends every company in the chain an SMS and email at the same time, so nobody has to wait on a reply to know the deal is moving.

FAQ

Good to know

What does it cost to join?

Nothing. Sign-up, listing surplus, AI chain search, and contact details for every company in a chain are all free — no subscription, no hidden fees.

Is any cash involved?

By default, no — it's a direct barter. If a company is open to a cash top-up to balance an uneven trade, that's marked on the listing. It's always optional.

How does the AI build a trade chain?

Every listing is a node in a network: what a company has, and what it wants in return. The agent searches that network for a path from what you have to what you need — direct if possible, through up to 10 companies if that's what it takes.

What if what I need isn't listed yet?

List your surplus and your need in your Account — it's added to the network immediately, and the agent will include it the next time anyone searches for a matching chain.

What do I need to receive SMS and email confirmations?

A verified phone number, a work email, and your company name on file — see the Notifications section above. Without these, you can still list, search, and browse, but B2B SWAP has no way to reach you when a trade is confirmed.

Are my listings and account visible from any device?

Yes. Accounts and listings are stored on the server, not in your browser — sign in with the same phone number from any device and you'll see the same account, the same listings, and the same trade history.

How fast can I actually get material?

The chain itself is built in seconds. From there, speed depends on how quickly each company in the chain confirms — typically faster than a standard supplier procurement cycle.
